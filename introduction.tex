\section{Introduction}

Under Solvency UK and Solvency II, firms are required to hold capital (the Solvency Capital Requirement, or SCR) which ensures that their basic own funds are positive with a 99.5\% probability over a one-year horizon. This brief note outlines a general framework for modelling risk in a one-year VaR framework and defines the different sources of risk with mathematical clarity under such a general setting. The concepts that we introduce are not necessarily new. Our aim however is to remove ambiguity and to recommend a common framework under which which, ignoring any operational constraints, all risk specialisms and actuarial modelling teams inside insurance companies can operate.

The rest of the paper continues as follows. In Section 2, we give a general description of the environment; in Section 3, we define the risks excluding model risk; in Section 4, we define model risk and in Section 5, we provide examples of how this framework applies to various product types.
