\begin{abstract}
We outline a generic framework under which to model Value-at-Risk ("VaR") over a one-year horizon, as required by Solvency UK and Solvency II in Europe. We decompose risks into different distinct categories, starting from first principles, and in particular that the model must coincide with the production of the balance sheet.
\end{abstract}
